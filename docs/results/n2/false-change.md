Windows of 256 px hidden at most 1 % on both dates, hidden pixels grown by 2 px and a 8 px border left out. Counts are pooled over windows before dividing. *Other ground* mixes true and false changes, for scale.

A pixel has changed where the moving image, matched to the reference's mean and standard deviation and resampled onto its grid, differs from it by more than 3 times the noise of the difference (Immerkær's estimator on each image). Every correction, no correction included, goes through the same resampling (Fourier x4 then cubic spline) and the same threshold.
